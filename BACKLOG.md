- [ ] WHY IT WASN'T BUILT, and what the next attempt needs. Best result reached was **518 → 359 frozen transitions (9.0% → 6.2%)**, audit still 0/13,967 — and it **broke `test:assumed-body`**, the item-2b safety invariant that a trainee who declined their body metrics is never prescribed more than one who gave them. A stated 55kg/52/female profile's Romanian Deadlift collapsed to **6kg, below an empty bar**, ending up lighter than the same lift for the declined profile at 16kg — a 2.7x gap between near-identical bodies, so structural, not physiological. Root cause is a flaw in my own design: **the pin makes the freeze self-perpetuating** — pinning this week's load to last week's means this week equals last week, so the lift is still "stuck", so it pins again, and the weight can never escape even once the estimate would afford a real step. The pin is not optional either: reps feed `prescribeLoad` as `repRangeLabel`, so bumping reps WITHOUT a pin lets the weight fall, and more reps at less weight is a deload wearing progress's clothes — invisible, because the frozen-week counter falls either way. Both pinned and unpinned versions fail `test:assumed-body`, for opposite reasons. THE SIXTH ATTEMPT should compute the load first with unbumped reps and only bump-and-re-derive when it actually resolves equal to last week's post-coherence load — the one ordering where the pin cannot manufacture a freeze. Four traps now named and avoidable: compare post-`enforceLoadCoherence` on BOTH sides (comparing pre- against post- made it *worse*, 380 → 272); pin to the NAME-keyed previous load, never `Math.min(name, slot)` (the slot value belongs to last week's variation — pinning to it held a rotated-in lift at the outgoing variation's weight and produced 9 `rotation_relative_load` failures); don't age a lift's streak once per slot (an accessory on two days aged twice, hit the cap in half the time and froze outright — tier3 went 77 → 191); and `shiftReps` has a floor but NO ceiling, so any accumulating bump needs its own cap or a strength block's 4-6 quietly becomes an endurance block.
