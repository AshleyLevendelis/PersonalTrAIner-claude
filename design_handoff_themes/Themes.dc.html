Turn 1Themes — 5 → 8, and light canvases that you can actually read

Why Daylight is hard to read today: three tokens never got a light value. Hero numbers are hard‑coded #E4FCF4 (near‑white on paper), the TrAIner's text is #DAD5FA in :root (lavender on paper), and the accent is used as text at 11–13px where it measures 3.8:1. Glow text‑shadows also haze small type on a light canvas. The fix below adds three tokens every theme must define — --primary-text, --num-hero, --role-ai-text — and tunes every light value to ≥ 4.5:1 for body/labels and ≥ 7:1 for secondary text. Ratios shown are computed live from the values.

1dThe rules that make every theme readable
Three new tokens, every theme defines them
--primary-text — the accent when it is words (links, "Nutrition ›", PR values, active tab label). Dark themes: same as --primary. Light: a step dark enough for 4.5:1.
--num-hero — replaces hard‑coded #E4FCF4. Light: an ink of the accent hue.
--role-ai-text / --role-ai-bg — moved out of :root into each theme.
Floors, checked by test-appearance.ts
foreground ≥ 12:1 · text‑tertiary ≥ 7:1 · muted‑foreground ≥ 4.5:1 (it's used at 11px) · primary‑text ≥ 4.5:1 · num‑hero ≥ 7:1 · role‑ai‑text ≥ 4.5:1 on role‑ai‑bg · primary‑foreground ≥ 4.5:1 on primary. The in‑app contrast guard measures --primary-text, not the fill.
Light canvas behaviour
Text glow (.glow-text, .glow-mint* on text) resolves to none — a light halo on paper is haze. Box glow on the CTA/FAB is kept at subtle. --hero-wash drops to .08. Grain stays inverted. --primary-foreground becomes per‑theme (white on Frost's blue, dark on Linen's amber).
Settings sheet
Theme cards split into two rows labelled Dark and Light; a 2×2 grid of 8 gets long. Add Match system as a first chip: picks the default dark (Nightshift) or default light (Daylight) from prefers-color-scheme. Accent chips show the resolved text step on light themes, so the swatch is honest about what a link will look like.
