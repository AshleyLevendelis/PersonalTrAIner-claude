What the app keeps

To build and adjust your training and meal plans, the app stores what you tell it and what you log: your profile answers (such as age, height, weight, goal, injuries, allergies and diet), your plan, the sets and sessions you log, meals, water, weigh-ins, steps, and your conversations with the coach. It is stored in our database so you can use the app on another phone after signing in.

Steps and heart rate from Apple Health and Health Connect

In the phone app, and only if you switch each one on in Profile, the app reads your step count and, separately, your heart rate and resting heart rate from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), which a watch or chest strap writes to. Steps fill in your daily steps; heart rate is used to show your average and highest heart rate after each session and your resting heart rate on Home. When you record a cardio workout (Start and End on the Exercise tab), it also reads the calories burned your phone or watch recorded between Start and End, and for a workout on foot the distance, to log that workout. If you switch on workouts from your watch, it reads the workouts your watch recorded in the last two days (their type, time, distance and calories) so it can offer to log them; nothing is logged unless you tap. If you switch on sleep from your watch, it reads how long you slept on each of the last eighteen nights, to show last night against your usual. Your sleep is worked out on your phone and is not saved to our database; a short summary (last night, your usual and the week) is sent with a message you send the coach, so it can take it into account. If you tap Check what your watch shares in Profile, it reads the kinds of data you have switched on, for up to the last seventeen days, only to count the records and note when they were written; that report is made on your phone, holds no readings, and is not saved or sent by the app. It reads those only. It does not read any other health data, and it never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. These figures are used only to show your progress and to coach you. They are never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone for marketing. You can switch either off in Profile, or remove the permission in your phone's Health settings, at any time.

The coach

When you chat with the coach, your message and the parts of your profile and plan it needs to answer are sent to Google's Gemini service to write the reply. A photo you send to the coach is read once to answer you and is not stored. The coach's replies are written by AI and can be wrong: the flag beside any reply reports it, and the reply and what you wrote are kept, and emailed to us, so it can be checked and put right.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show advertising, and your data is never used for it.
  • We do not share your health information with anyone except the services needed to run the app, as described above.

Your control

On Profile, under Your data, you can download everything the app has stored about you as one file, or delete it all. Deleting removes your plan, meals, weigh-ins, logged sets, steps and chat history, and cannot be undone.

Not medical advice

The app gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It does not diagnose or treat anything. For pain, an injury, a medical condition or pregnancy, it points you to a doctor, physio or midwife.
