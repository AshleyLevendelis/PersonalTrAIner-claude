USER:  Full form guide
COACH: [Watch Barbell Deadlift Tutorial on YouTube](www.youtube.com/results?search_query=barbell+deadlift+tutorial+form)

### Setup & Stance
- **Foot position**: Walk up to the bar and place your feet hip-width apart. The barbell should sit directly over the middle of your feet, about an inch away from your shins.
- **The grip**: Hinge at your hips and bend your knees just enough to grip the bar just outside your shins. Keep your arms completely straight — they act as chains, not levers.

### The Pull (Execution)
- **Set your back**: Pull your chest up and push your hips back to flatten your spine. Think about squeezing your armpits tight (like you're trying to crush oranges under them) to engage your lats.
- **Take the slack out**: Pull up slightly on the bar until you hear it click against the weights. This creates tension before the lift starts.
- **Push the floor**: Do not yank the bar off the ground. Drive your feet hard into the floor, pushing the earth away, and stand up by driving your hips forward. Keep the bar touching your legs the entire way up.

### The Lockout & Descent
- **Lockout**: Stand tall, squeezing your glutes and quads at the top. Do not lean back or hyperextend your lower back.
- **The descent**: Reverse the movement by pushing your hips back first. Once the bar clears your knees, bend your legs to lower it back to the floor. Reset completely before the next rep.

### Common Mistakes
- **Rounding the back**: This usually happens if you start with the bar too far forward or fail to brace your core. Keep the bar close.
- **Bending the arms**: Pulling with your biceps can cause serious injury. Keep your arms dead straight throughout.
- **Squatting the weight**: If your hips are too low, your shins will push the bar forward. Keep your hips higher than your knees.
