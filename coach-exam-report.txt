The Lift
1. Push the floor away — Do not try to yank the bar up with your arms. Think about leg pressing the floor away from you.
2. Bar path — Keep the bar in contact with your shins and thighs the entire way up. It should travel in a straight vertical line.
3. Lockout — Stand tall by squeezing your glutes at the top. Do not lean back or hyperextend your lower back.

The Descent
