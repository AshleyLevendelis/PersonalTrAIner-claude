1. Setup: Feet hip-width apart, bar resting right over the middle of your feet, an inch from your shins.
2. Grip: Hinge at the hips to reach down and grip the bar just outside your knees with straight arms.
3. Position: Pull your chest up slightly to flatten your back, and pull your shoulder blades back down toward your back pockets to engage your lats.
4. Brace: Take a deep breath into your belly and brace your core like you're about to get punched.
