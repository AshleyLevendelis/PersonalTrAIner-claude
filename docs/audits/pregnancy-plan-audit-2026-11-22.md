# Pregnant exercise plans, audited (22 Nov 2026)

His request: "do audit on a pregnant womans exercise plan what exercises does she get?" Report only, nothing changed.

## How
Four plans printed in full (below), then 96 plans measured: full gym, home gym, dumbbells and bodyweight; three styles;
beginner to advanced; due in 24 weeks or in 10. Real generator, seeded.

## What holds (the rules he has ruled on)
- No jumping, nothing lying flat on the back or front, no hard ab moves (crunches, leg raises, rollouts): none found.
- Every working set 8+ reps; effort RPE 5-7 (7 at most from 28 weeks); no heavy strength blocks.
- Pelvic floor exercises in the warm-up of every training day: 6,144 of 6,144.
- From 28 weeks, planks, push-ups, lunges, split squats and single-leg RDLs are swapped out.

## What does not
1. **The plan carries on past the due date.** All 48 plans due in 10 weeks keep prescribing pregnancy training up to 6
   weeks after the baby is due (16-week plan, nothing ends it). After a birth, training restarts only after the
   6-8 week postnatal check. What the app does at the due date is his decision (medical band).
2. **Face-down and twisting work still reaches late pregnancy** (slot-weeks from 28 weeks, of 96 plans): Renegade Row
   240 (a plank with a row: the plank is already banned, this version is not), Reverse Nordic Curl 144 (kneeling
   lean-back, loads the stretched stomach wall), Cossack Squat 32 (a deep side lunge: the side lunge is banned, this is
   not), loaded rotation (Landmine Rotation 132, Rotary Torso Machine 32), and standing single-leg calf raises 856
   (single-leg balance work is banned, these are not; fine holding a wall). CSCS, mine to fix.
3. **Before 28 weeks the core work is ambitious**: Copenhagen Plank 184, Archer Push-Ups 228, Pike Push-Ups 148 (head
   below the hips), Decline Push-Ups 36. Guidance allows what feels fine, but nothing tells her to stop if her stomach
   domes or cones down the middle, the standard sign to stop that exercise. Worth a line on those moves.
