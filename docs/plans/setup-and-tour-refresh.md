are asking. Let's make the wording more enthusiastic/friendly. 2. The tour, the app does way more than the tour shows

## Part 1: setup

### Getting more value from the same questions (no extra screens: his 14 Nov "setup should get shorter" stands)

1. **A short "why I'm asking" under each question.** One small grey line saying what the answer changes ("This sets
   your calorie target"). People answer more carefully when they can see it matters. It is also honest: every line
   names something the app really does with the answer.
2. **Plain words where a wrong answer costs the most.** Experience, recovery and daily activity change the plan and the
   calories more than anything else, and their current labels use trainer words ("Novice", "recovery capacity") that
   people guess at.
3. **A friendly line between the two halves.** "Training's sorted 💪 Now the food side." Setup feels shorter when it
   has a middle.
4. **Optional, one more screen (his call):** after the goal, "Anything specific you're aiming for? A first pull-up, a
   wedding, a race." Typed, skippable, saved for the coach. It is the single most motivating thing a person can tell a
   coach, but it breaks "no longer". Recommended only if he wants it.

### Answer choices worth changing (plain words, same meaning)

- **Experience:** Beginner (unchanged) · **Novice → "Some experience"**, "Training fairly regularly for 6+ months" ·
  **Intermediate → "Experienced"**, "2+ years, comfortable with the big lifts" · Advanced, "Years in, progress comes
  slowly now". "Novice" sits next to "Beginner" and people can't tell them apart.
- **Recovery:** Stretched thin, "Short on sleep, lots of stress, or a physical job" · Getting by, "Decent sleep most
  nights, stress is manageable" · Well rested, "Sleeping well and feeling good".
- **Cardio:** Love it, "Bring it on" · It's fine, "I'll do what the plan needs" · Not for me, "Just the minimum,
  please".
- **Style:** Hybrid, "A bit of everything" (was "Best of everything", which reads as a promise).
- **Goal:** Fat loss, "Lose fat, keep your muscle" · Muscle growth, "Build size and strength" (unchanged) ·
  Functional strength, "Get stronger and move better" · Conditioning, "Fitter, with more stamina".

Every "why" line is checked against what the app does: none promises a result, and "filter" (not "guarantee") stays
on the diet line, as his allergen rules require.

## Part 2: the tour

### Today: 10 stops, about a minute

Welcome · Home · the rings · Nutrition · meals · Exercise · log a practice set · Tools (timers) · the settings gear ·
the chat. Skip is on every stop, and the tour can be replayed from settings.

### What it never shows (the app's best bits)

**Exercise**
- **About this exercise:** tap any exercise name for How to (steps and a YouTube search), Muscles and History.
- **The ⋮ menu:** swap, remove, move, add an exercise or ban one for good. Every change can be undone.
- **Too easy / About right / Too hard** after a lift: the next session's weight follows the answer.
- **The day menu:** move a session, mark it missed, make today a rest day, or do the shortest version.
- **Week and Program:** every day's session on one screen, and the whole plan as a calendar.

**Tools**
- **Plate calculator:** type a bar weight and see the plates for each side.
- **Timers keep running** at the bottom of every screen while you use the rest of the app.

**Nutrition**
- **The day strip:** see and swap any of the next six days' meals, and add a day to the shopping list.
- **Swap, heart or eat out:** swap a meal, heart one you love, or mark a meal as eaten out and the rest of the day
  adjusts.
- **The shopping list:** built from your meals, by aisle.

**Home**
- **Weight history:** tap Weight for your weekly averages and a graph.

**Profile (behind the gear)**
- **Allergies,** your kit, **pause my plan** (holiday or illness), and how often the coach messages you.

**The chat**
- **Things to try:** "swap my squats", "I'm eating out tonight", a photo of a meal or a menu, "open my shopping list".
- **The flag** beside a coach reply reports it.

### How to fit them in: DECIDED 22 Nov 2026, his ruling "Short tour, then offer more" (option A)

- **A. A short tour, then "Want the full tour?" (recommended).** The 10 stops stay as they are. The last stop offers
  "Show me everything (about 2 minutes)" or "Maybe later". Both tours can be replayed from settings, and Skip is on
  every stop.
- **B. One long tour** of about 25 stops. Everything in one go, but most people will skip halfway and miss the chat
  stop at the end.
- **C. Tips as you go.** The first time you open a screen or an exercise, one small tip appears there ("Tap the name
  for how to do it"). Nothing up front, but it is slower to discover, and it is the most work to build.

What every option keeps: Skip on every stop, a "3 of 18" counter, nothing claimed that the app does not do (the tour's
existing honesty check covers every new line), and the practice set still removed when the tour moves on.
