# Pregnancy as one switch (his rulings 15 Nov 2026)

The browser test (Priya, 20 weeks pregnant) found the app holding three things that disagreed: the "Pregnant" tick
(exercise rules only, his 14 Nov ruling), a fat-loss target of 1,448 kcal (a 363 kcal deficit), and the coach's own
notes saying weight loss isn't appropriate. His rulings, each from three options:

1. **"Maintenance + midwife"**: while Pregnant is ticked there is no deficit and no surplus. Fat loss and muscle
   gain count as maintenance for calories and macros; the goal itself is kept and comes back when unticked. The
   screen says "Check your calories with your midwife or GP." The app never adds pregnancy extra calories (the
   midwife's number). It REPLACES the "calories untouched" half of his 14 Nov "Exercise rules only".
2. **"NHS list, auto-on"**: a food rule switched on by the tick, never chosen or saved as a diet. Kept out (tag
   `pregnancy_avoid`, both food tables): cold cured meats (chorizo, salami, pepperoni), liver, cold-smoked fish
   (smoked salmon), raw shellfish (oysters). The table holds no pâté, mould-ripened or soft blue cheese, shark,
   swordfish or marlin today; any added later must carry the tag. Watched over the week, never banned: tuna (4
   medium tins, or 2 steaks) and oily fish (2 portions of about 140 g). Fine and NOT flagged: cooked ham, pre-packed
   cooked meats, pasteurised and hard cheeses (mozzarella, cheddar, feta, parmesan), cooked shellfish.
3. **"Ask due date"**: one optional date under the tick (migration `due_date`). The app works out the weeks. From 28
   weeks: working sets capped one notch lower (RPE 7, was 8) and no new heavier weights (the progression holds the
   load). Nothing more clinical. Without a date, every pregnancy is treated as past 16 weeks, as now.

Also: the chat offers the tick when someone says they're pregnant (never ticks it silently); the coach is told the
target is maintenance and sends calorie questions to the midwife or GP; the weekly note drops "while you're in a
deficit"; "Leave it as it is" no longer leaves a line saying the effort cap was applied.

## Review (five questions)
1. Training effect: unchanged before 28 weeks; after, one notch less effort and no load increases, which is the
   ACOG/RCOG direction (moderate intensity, talk test) and still trains.
2. Takes away: progression in the third trimester (by design); deficit nutrition (by his ruling).
3. Fundamentals: patterns and frequency untouched.
4. Floors: RPE 8 health ceiling unchanged; 7 applies only past 28 weeks with a due date.
5. Scope: food safety list is the NHS public list, not clinical nutrition; calories go to the midwife.
