# The phone app, with steps from the phone (20 Nov 2026)

His request: "Start on the App Store version with step counting."

## What it is

The same web app, built by `npm run build`, inside a thin native shell (Capacitor 8). That gives a real App Store and
Play Store listing and one native feature to start: today's steps read from Apple Health on an iPhone and Health
Connect on Android. Nothing about the website changes: every new piece checks whether it is running inside the phone
app first, and the phone-only code is loaded only there (the website's first download grew by 580 bytes).

## Built here

- `capacitor.config.ts`, `ios/`, `android/` (generated by `npx cap add`, then edited):
  - Android declares READ_STEPS and REMOVES the 46 other health permissions the plugin declares by default (Google
    Play refuses unused health permissions). Minimum Android 8, which Health Connect needs. Health Connect's
    "Privacy policy" link points at `/privacy.html`.
  - iPhone: the Health permission sentence (steps only, never writes), and the HealthKit entitlement on both builds.
- `src/lib/health-steps.ts`: ask the phone for steps (read only), read today's total (local midnight to now; the phone
  removes the double count of phone plus watch), save it into the existing `daily_steps` row with source
  `healthkit` / `health_connect` (the column was made for this in August, so no database change).
- Home reads the phone's count when it opens and whenever the app comes back to the front; a line under the ring says
  "From Apple Health". Profile, Personal TrAIner & app, "Steps from your phone": on / off, only in the phone app.
- `public/privacy.html`: a DRAFT privacy page (store listings and Health Connect need one). Ashley approves the words and
  adds a contact email before any submission.
- The offline helper (service worker) is not started inside the phone app: the whole app ships in the install.

## Decisions (mine, recorded)

- **Which number a day keeps:** the phone's count replaces an earlier phone count, and never lowers a number he typed.
  A phone left in a bag undercounts; a typed number is his statement about his day. The higher is kept, and the open
  steps box says so.
- **Steps only, read only.** Asking for less is what Apple and Google review hardest, and it is all this needs.
  Weight, workouts and heart rate are later, each its own decision.
- **The switch is kept on the phone, not the account**: it is a fact about this phone's permission.
- **An iPhone never says "denied" for a read** (Apple's privacy rule: a refused read looks like zero steps). So a
  finished permission sheet counts as on, and the sheet says where to look if the count stays at 0.

## Not built, named

- Building and signing the apps: needs Xcode on a Mac (iPhone) and Android Studio (Android). This cloud session has
  neither. `docs/handover/phone-app-first-build.md` is the prompt for his machine.
- Real-phone proof: the browser tests use a pretend health store. Whether Apple's and Google's sheets appear and real
  counts arrive is proven only on a phone.
- The app's id `app.personaltrainer.coach` is a placeholder until the first store upload, permanent after it.
- App icons and the launch screen are Capacitor's defaults.
- Push notifications: Android BUILT 23 Nov 2026 (src/lib/native-push.ts, supabase/functions/_shared/fcm.ts,
  docs/handover/android-notifications.md); iPhone not built (needs an Apple push key).
- Store listings, screenshots, the age rating and the health-data forms (Apple's privacy labels, Google's Health
  Connect declaration).

## Checks

- `test:phone-app` (31 checks, 9/9 mutations): the native settings ask for steps and nothing else, derived from the
  plugin's own manifest so a plugin update that adds a permission fails it; the iPhone sentence and entitlement; the
  code's single permission request; no static import of the plugin; the privacy page's claims; which number a day keeps.
- `verify:health-steps` (22 checks, 11/11 meaningful mutations): Profile's switch only in the phone app, steps-only
  request, a refusal and a missing Health Connect said plainly, Home filling its ring, a typed bigger number kept, the
  phone taking over once it passes it, nothing read while switched off.
