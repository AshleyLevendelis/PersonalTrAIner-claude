## What was wrong, and what changed

### H14 — wrong buttons under a question; M2 — a re-asked question lost its buttons
**Wrong.** The coach's words and its chips arrive separately — usually from two
different model calls, because a turn that records an answer comes back with tool
calls and no prose, and a second call writes the sentence. Nothing checked they agreed,
so "how old are you, and what are your current height and weight?" got the meals chips.
Separately, a guard that stopped duplicate chip grids *dropped* a re-asked question's
card whenever an older card for it was still unanswered — so after a detour the only
chips were on the first asking, off screen.

**Changed.**
- The app reads the coach's sentence and shows a card only when that sentence asks for
  it (`src/lib/onboarding-chip-match.ts`). If the model asked for chips for a different
  question and the sentence plainly asks exactly one other open question, that card is
  shown instead (the H14 turn now gets the age / height / weight boxes). Otherwise: no
  card, and the typing box still works. Wrong chips are now impossible; missing chips
  are possible.
- One live card per question, on the newest message that asks it. A re-ask moves the
  card down (the old copy is flagged, not deleted — the stall-breaker counts asks by
  reading it) and the move survives a reload. A re-asked question takes its card along
  even if the model forgot to ask for it.
- `onboarding-chat`: a `present_slot` from a model call that said nothing is answered
  "not shown", so the next call asks for the chips that fit its own sentence; chips
  from the call that wrote the reply are sent first. Later-call `record_context_fact` /
  `record_goal` were being deduped on a `slot_key` they do not carry, so a second,
  different fact was silently lost — fixed.
- Two comments described a chip backstop that did not exist (`onboarding-chat/index.ts`,
  `reply-resolver.ts`). Both now say exactly what stands behind the model.

**Decided unprompted, reversible.**
- A first asking where the model simply forgot `present_slot` still gets no chips.
  Inventing a menu from prose alone would be the removed forced-chips call by another
  route.
- **Deviation from the tracer's proposal, flagged:** the tracer said to *drop* a silent
  call's chip request. It is kept as a fallback behind the speaking call's instead.
  About a third of round trips end on the tools-less call, which cannot ask for chips
  at all; dropping would lose chips that were right. The client check stops wrong ones
  either way.

### M4 — tapping a summary row looked dead
**Wrong.** The prompt was appended to the conversation while the summary stayed open
under it, so it rendered above a card taller than the screen, and each tap stacked
another copy. Reproduced before fixing: three taps, three copies, 900px above the fold.

**Changed.** Tapping a row puts the summary away and shows one prompt with its options
as the last thing on screen. "Review and build my plan" goes back unchanged. Answering
brings the summary straight back at the row that changed, with no trip to the coach.
If the change opens a new question (a barbell tier makes the working-lifts question
apply) the coach asks it at the bottom and the summary returns once it is answered. A
typed answer still goes through the coach, and the edit is closed when that turn ends
whatever came back, so nobody is stranded. A reload mid-edit returns to the summary.

### L2 — ticks without units
"Heaviest dumbbells — 24 kg", "Height — 178 cm", "Weight — 82 kg", in the tick, the
summary row, and the list the coach is sent (one function for all three). Every
numeric answer except age. The dumbbell / kettlebell / bag input labels gained "(kg)".

### L3 — the two edit prompts (only)
"Sure — pick a different equipment." / "Sure — type what you'd like foods to avoid to
be instead." are now "Sure — let's change that." followed by the question's own
wording, plus "Type it in below." where the answer is typed. Checked for all 31
questions. **The allergy tick ("… will be kept out of every meal") was not touched.**

### L5 — "3 meals and one snack" ticked as "3 meals"
The plan did include a snack, but only because snacks default to on. "3 meals, no
snacks" with the same miss would have built a plan *with* a snack and said nothing.
The app now reads a plain snack answer from what was typed and ticks "Meals a day —
3 meals + a snack" (or ", no snacks"). Deliberately narrow: a message that mentions
snacking twice, or both ways, is left to the coach. The summary now always lists
Snacks, since a default nobody can see is one nobody can turn off.

### L6 — two goals from one sentence
Goals the coach notes are merged when they say nearly the same thing (lower bar when
they came from the same sentence, or share a figure like "80kg"); the measurable one is
kept; a second target weight replaces the first. Two genuinely different goals from one
sentence are both kept. Whether an inferred goal should be *confirmed* first is
unchanged and is an owner question below.

### L7 — the tour contradicted itself
Step 2 said "Home shows; it never logs." one card before step 3 said water, steps and
the weigh-in "are logged right here". Step 2 was stale (written 3 Sep when Home was a
read-out; Home began logging on 6 Sep). Confirmed from `Dashboard.tsx`. Now: "Home
shows your training; it never logs a set." The comment above step 3 was a day out of
date too and is corrected.

### M3 — combat still offered
Hidden from onboarding chips, from the options the onboarding coach is sent, from new
answers (tapped, typed, or mapped by the coach from "I box" — refused out loud with the
three real options put back), from the Profile picker for everyone *not* already on it,
and from the coach's style-change proposal. **Nothing is migrated.** A profile already
on combat keeps its style, its plan, its label, and regenerates as before; the type,
the engine config, the catalogue tags and the gates that build combat plans are
untouched. A half-finished onboarding draft saved earlier that holds combat is asked
the style question again. There is no "classic questionnaire" left in the codebase to
hide it from.

`chat-gemini`: the one edit this lane was permitted there, kept to the option lists —
the tool description, its enum and the enum's description, the "one of …" instruction,
plus one clause saying combat is not a style to move *to*.

### M1 — a mid-setup question was answered in full
The prompt's deferral rule covered only trivia; the scope rules beside it said to
answer training and nutrition questions "substantively". Now: one warm sentence that
it is a good question and will be answered properly once setup is done, **with the
reason** (the answer depends on what the coach is about to learn), then straight back
to the interrupted question with its chips. A worked example of the right reply sits
beside the wrong one. Never parked, listed by name: pain, medication, extreme targets,
disordered eating, allergies and food safety, a question about the question just asked,
and where something is in the app.

**The cap is counted by the app**, not the model: the client sends how many have been
parked; the prompt is told "0 of 2", and at two is told to stop parking and answer
briefly.

**"Then come back to it" is real.** The coach calls a new `park_question` tool; the app
keeps the question in the person's own words; the first coach message after the plan is
built ends "You asked me something while we were setting up — it's right below. Tap it
and I'll answer it properly", with the question as the first thing to tap.

### Reported, not changed: the onboarding prompt contradicts itself on allergens
The shared honesty block (`_shared/coach-rules.ts`, also inline in `chat-gemini`) says
only seven allergens have a tag check and that celery, sesame, mustard, lupin and
sulphites "have NO tag mechanism at all". The onboarding paragraph below it says all
twelve are enforced. **From the code, the second is true**: `diet-rules.ts` forbids all
twelve tags, the food data carries them, and `generate-meals` has a rule for each, since
commit `dbc4c3a6` (27 Aug 2026). So the *shared* block is the stale one, and the main
coach is telling people with those five allergies that nothing checks for them. That
under-claims rather than over-claims, but it is untrue, and it is allergen wording:
safety-adjacent, plan first, owner's words. Nothing was reworded.
