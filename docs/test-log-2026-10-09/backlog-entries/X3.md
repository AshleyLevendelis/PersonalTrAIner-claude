- [x] **"ADD AN EXERCISE" OFFERS WHAT THE DAY IS FOR — BUILT 9 Oct 2026 (tester's M13).** The
  tester, on leg day: *"suggests Arm Circles and two band moves: 'Your shoulders gets the least
  work in this session'."*
  **REPRODUCED FIRST**, on the untouched tree, with the tracer's eight seeded plans for her profile
  (limited kit, shoulder flag): 8 of 8 leg days had a non-leg move in the top three, and seed b
  printed her screen word for word (Arm Circles, Band Face Pulls, Band Pull-Aparts).
  **WHAT WAS WRONG.** The list ranks by "the muscle this session works least". The session's
  muscles were counted from every row on the day, and a shoulder flag puts a two-set shoulder-care
  warm-up drill on every day — so on leg day "shoulders" was a muscle the day trained, the thinnest
  one, and exactly what the ranking rewards. Warm-up drills were themselves offered as additions.
  Looking at the other three days of the same plans found the same fault by two more doors:
  "Shoulders & Abs" was led by seven hamstring moves (its one light leg accessory, there for the
  week's sake, made hamstrings the thinnest muscle), and a chest day's only offer was "Jump Rope —
  trains shoulders". And the verb never agreed with a plural muscle.
  **DECIDED AS A CSCS COACH** (the brief delegated it; basis with each):
  1. *Movement prep is not work.* The session's theme is read from its working exercises only, and
     a warm-up drill is never offered as an addition (search still reaches one). Basis: an added
     exercise extends the training stimulus; prep is dosed to prepare, not to be added to.
  2. *Nor is the week's light leg accessory on a non-leg day* (`isWeekSupportLeg`, builder E2's own
     "not a second leg day"). Same basis.
  3. *The day's purpose first.* A candidate that IS the work the day's name promises (E2's
     defining patterns, asked through `dayHoldsDefiningWork`) sorts above one that merely shares a
     muscle with the session. Basis: specificity — what belongs on leg day is leg work.
  4. *A flagged area is not a gap to fill.* With a shoulder or lower-back flag, nothing that leads
     with the muscle on that area is suggested, that muscle is never "the least work", and it is
     not a reason for anything else to be listed. Basis: the plan already holds the dose judged
     right for a flagged joint; the app does not volunteer more loading on it. She can still pick
     one by search (her 13 Sep "show everything, warn me"), and the plan's own dose is untouched.
     **The other six areas (knee, hip, ankle, elbow, wrist, neck) get no extra rule**: they have no
     muscle group of their own here, and the catalogue's contraindication tags — already applied
     to the pool — are the whole answer. A wider rule ("nothing the catalogue marks as good FOR a
     flagged joint") was built and taken out the same hour: it removed leg curls for a bad knee and
     trunk work for a bad back, which is the opposite of what a coach offers. A check holds that a
     knee flag still gets hamstring and glute work on leg day.
  5. *"Gets the least work" is only said when it is true* — when that muscle has fewer sets than
     another the session works. On a day where everything was level, every row said it.
  6. *Among candidates alike in every other way, one she can add weight to comes first.* The tie
     used to fall to the alphabet (Air Squat, Bodyweight Good Morning, Box Squat (Bodyweight) ahead
     of Goblet Squats for a lifter with dumbbells). Basis: added work should be work that can
     progress; her 18 Sep ruling on the swap list is the same call ("weight always wins").
  The sentence: "Your shoulders get…", "Your chest gets…" — a switch with no default
  (`leastWorkVerb`), so a new muscle group cannot be added without choosing its verb.
  File: `src/lib/exercise-add-candidates.ts` only. The coach's "add an exercise" resolves a NAMED
  movement (`resolveAdditionRequest`) and is unaffected.
  **CSCS REVIEW.** (1) Training effect: nothing prescribed changes — this is the order and content
  of a list of suggestions; whatever is chosen is still added through the same path with the same
  settle tail. (2) What it takes away: prep drills and flagged-area work from the suggested list;
  both remain reachable by search. **On her chest day the suggested list is now EMPTY** (every
  chest and triceps move her kit and shoulder allow is already in the session) and the sheet shows
  its existing sentence, "Nothing left that fits… Search below". Before, that day offered carries
  and shrugs because a warm-up drill made "back" part of chest day. (3) Fundamentals: untouched.
  (4) No floor or ceiling is redefined. (5) In scope — no rehab or diagnosis; point 4 is a rule
  about what the app volunteers, and the pain rule is untouched.
  **VERIFIED.** `test:log-manners` §5 (the eight seeded leg days, every day of four of the plans, a
  full-gym flagged plan, a knee-flagged plan, a healthy control). Mutations for this section: 11,
  11 caught — one only after the gate gained a fixture: on her kit nothing shoulder-led survived to
  be offered anyway, so removing the lead-muscle rule changed nothing (MISSED). Measured rather
  than assumed redundant: a full gym does hold one (Face Pulls on a back day, Landmine Press on a
  chest day), so that plan is pinned by seed and the rule kept. `test:exercise-add` and
  `test:edit-keeps-the-bar` pass unchanged.
  **NOT VERIFIED.** The list was not read on a real screen after the change (`verify:exercise-add`
  drives the default full-gym, no-injury plan — it passes, and it never reaches this). Purpose-first
  uses the day's DEFINING patterns, so on "Legs & Calves" squats, hinges and single-leg work sort
  above calf raises and leg curls, which are not defining; whether accessories of the day's own
  muscles should sit in the same band is a judgement left as it is.

- [x] **THE WEIGHT CARRIES DOWN; TIGHT HIPS NEVER DOUBLE A DRILL — BUILT 9 Oct 2026 (tester's M11,
  M9).**
  **M11.** *"Logged Box Squat set 1 at 22.5 kg, ticked set 2 blank. Set 2 saved as Bodyweight."*
  Reproduced on a real screen before the fix: receipts "Set 1: 10 reps @ 22.5kg" / "Set 2: 10 reps
  · Bodyweight", and the stored row `0kg, bodyweight`. A blank weight box fell back to typed → last
  week → the plan, never to the set just done; on a lift with no planned weight that can be done
  unloaded, the fallback is 0 and 0 is filed as bodyweight.
  **DECIDED AS A CSCS COACH — use the carried weight, do not ask.** Straight sets repeat the load
  unless the plan says otherwise, so the set just done is the best evidence there is of what the
  next one weighs — better than last week's number, and better than the plan's once she has
  departed from it on this exercise today. A blank box on set 2 logs set 1's weight; a changed
  weight on set 2 is what set 3 carries. Asking instead ("type the weight") would put a refusal in
  front of the commonest tap in the app for a question whose answer is on the row above. A log
  that says 22.5 / bodyweight / 22.5 understates the work and hands progression a false zero.
  - **The exception is a plan that STEPS the load** between the two sets (a ramped or pyramid
    prescription): there the plan's own number stands.
  - The bodyweight flag carries with it, and on a belt lift (pull-ups, dips) it is the ADDED weight
    that carries.
  - **Not in calibration week on sets 2+**, which deliberately have no default (her 10 Sep ruling):
    there the row still refuses a blank box and offers the next-weight chips.
  - A build-up row keeps the prescription's number and a drop keeps its step down, as before.
  - **A genuinely bodyweight exercise is unaffected**: with nothing loaded above it, a blank tick
    is bodyweight, as before (driven: two blank ticks, two "Bodyweight" receipts, no new line).
  It is shown as the box's faint number (the app's convention — a blank tick logs what the box
  shows), and because a hint must say whose it is (her 18 Sep "last time" ruling), a line under the
  row says **"same as set 1 · 22.5kg"** — only where the carry changed the number, so a session
  lifted exactly as written gains nothing. One resolver now feeds the save, the placeholder, the
  plate calculator and the tour's "needs a weight" marker, so the box cannot show one number and
  log another. **Decided unprompted, reversible (words are Ashley's):** the line "same as set N ·
  22.5kg" / "· +10kg" / "· bodyweight" (one function in `coach-voice.ts`).
  **CSCS REVIEW (M11).** (1) Training effect: nothing prescribed changes; what is RECORDED after a
  blank tick changes, and progression reads the record — a carried 22.5 where a false bodyweight
  set (or last week's lighter number) used to be written. That is the point. (2) Takes away: on a
  straight-set lift the plan's printed number, and last week's, no longer fill a blank box once a
  set has been logged at a different weight today. Deliberate. (3) Fundamentals: progressive
  overload is better served by a true log. (4) No floor or ceiling redefined; calibration's "no
  default" is kept and pinned. (5) In scope.
  **M9.** *"Anything feeling tight? → Hips adds World's Greatest Stretch and Bodyweight Squat to
  Stand, both already in the warm-up. Says 'do these first' but lists them last. Warm-up grows from
  6 to 10 min."* Reproduced on the harness's copy of her leg day: the block listed World's Greatest
  Stretch, Hip Airplanes and Bodyweight Squat to Stand under a warm-up that already held two of
  them, drawn below Mobility. The picker chose from the whole catalogue with no knowledge of the
  day's warm-up.
  **DECIDED AS A CSCS COACH.**
  - *Never the same drill twice in one warm-up.* A drill already there is skipped and the next best
    for that area is taken.
  - *An area the warm-up already prepares gets ONE new drill, not three,* and the ones already
    there are named: **"Your warm-up already has World's Greatest Stretch and Bodyweight Squat to
    Stand for your hips."** Basis: she has told the app something the plan could not know, so the
    answer is not "nothing"; but extra mobility should cost minutes only where it adds something.
    Her warm-up now goes from 6 minutes to 7. Where every drill for the area is already there,
    nothing is added and the sentence is the whole answer.
  - *Order: the general pulse-raiser, then what feels tight, then the session's own mobility.*
    Basis: raise temperature before mobilising (RAMP: raise, activate/mobilise, potentiate) — a
    joint moves through more range once it is warm. The line now says where they are: **"…do this
    straight after the general warm-up."** With no general block they lead, and it says "first".
  **Decided unprompted, reversible (words are Ashley's):** the "already has" sentence, "straight
  after the general warm-up", and "this" for one drill.
  **CLAUDE.md needs one line corrected by whoever merges** (builders may not edit it): the 15 Sep
  entry says the drills "go in at the **front** of TODAY's warm-up". They now go after the general
  block and before the session's mobility, for the reason above.
  **CSCS REVIEW (M9).** (1) Training effect: warm-up only — no set, weight or exercise changes.
  (2) Takes away: up to two duplicate drills (three minutes she was not getting anything for).
  (3) Fundamentals untouched. (4) The cap of three extra drills is unchanged; a second, smaller cap
  (one, for an area already prepared) is new and named. (5) In scope: tight is not hurt, and the
  hand-over to the pain triage is untouched.
  Files: new `src/lib/set-row.ts`, `SetGrid.tsx`, `coach-voice.ts`, `tightness.ts`,
  `WarmupSection.tsx`, `TodayPanel.tsx`.
  **VERIFIED.** `test:log-manners` §1 and §4. `verify:log-manners` §1 (22.5 on set 1, set 2's box
  shows 22.5 with its line, a blank tick, the receipt and the stored row; 25 on set 3 carries to
  set 4; a second lift ticked blank twice stays bodyweight) and §4 (her leg day: one new drill,
  none repeated, the "already has" sentence, the block drawn between General and Mobility).
  Screenshots read. `verify:tightness`, `verify:one-number` ("last time"), `verify:calibration-
  search` and `verify:absurd-weight` — see the gate list below.
  **NOT VERIFIED.** A real phone. The belt-lift carry and a stepped prescription on a screen (logic
  gate only — the harness has neither today). The coach cannot ask "what feels tight" (unchanged:
  `screen only`).
